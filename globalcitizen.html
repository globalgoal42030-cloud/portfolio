Features

Built for impact.

Every feature is designed to lower the barrier to civic action. Users earn points and rewards for completing verified actions — signing petitions, attending events, contacting representatives — which unlock access to exclusive concerts, experiences, and content.

🌍
Action Campaigns
Structured campaigns tied to each SDG goal — users complete tasks and earn verified impact points.
📊
Impact Tracker
Live community dashboard showing collective actions taken, goals met, and progress toward 2030 targets.
🎟
Rewards System
Points unlock tickets, merchandise, and experiences — civic action has real tangible benefits.
📅
Event Listings
Global and local events tied to SDG goals — rallies, clean-ups, summits, and live concerts.
🗳
Advocacy Tools
One-click petition signing, rep contact forms, and shareable pledge cards for social amplification.
🏆
Leaderboards
City, country, and global rankings — friendly competition drives engagement and sustained participation.
