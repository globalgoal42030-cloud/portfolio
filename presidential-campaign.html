★   The Candidate

Born to Serve,
Built to Lead

"I grew up in a family that worked three jobs and still struggled to make rent. I know what America is really asking for."

Marcus J. Rivera was born in San Antonio, Texas to working-class parents. A Yale Law graduate and former public defender, he served two terms as Mayor of Austin — turning it into a national model for progressive urban policy — before winning his Senate seat in 2022 with the largest margin in state history.

He is the husband of Dr. Elena Rivera and father of three. He coaches Little League on weekends. He believes democracy works best when everyone participates.
