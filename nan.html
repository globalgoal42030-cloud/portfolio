Overview

Design for justice.

The goal was to build a digital presence as powerful as the organization's voice — bold typography, clear calls to action, and seamless pathways to donate, join, and act. The site needed to serve both longtime members and first-time visitors arriving from social media campaigns.

Mission

Four pillars.

01
One Standard of Justice
Fighting for equal treatment under the law regardless of race, religion, ethnicity, or economic status.
02
Criminal Justice Reform
Advocating for accountability in policing, ending mass incarceration, and protecting civil liberties.
03
Voter Protection
Defending the right to vote through registration drives, legal challenges, and legislative advocacy.
04
Economic Equity
Pushing for fair wages, equal opportunity hiring, and community investment in underserved areas.
